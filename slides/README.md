# Presentation slides

`template.pptx` is the shared template for the talk: 15 min + 3 min Q&A, slides due **5 Nov, 6:00 pm**.

## Who edits what

The template has one section per member (see the Sections pane in PowerPoint). Edit only your own section; add as many slides as you need by duplicating the blank slide in it.

| Section | Owner | Time | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title + A · Introduction & methods | A Zhongjie | ~3 min | Motivation, research questions, models and SUMO build, validation |
| 01 · Highway | B Guowei | ~3 min | Ring-road results (RQ1) |
| 02 · City network | C Yixiang | ~3 min | Network results (RQ2) |
| 03 · Planning | D Daniel | ~2.5 min | Planning scenarios (RQ3) |
| 04 · Synthesis + Thank you | E Hongfang | ~3 min | Comparison with real data, overall conclusions |

Leave about 30 s of slack; aim for 1–1.5 min per content slide.

## Style

- Use the layouts in the template (Home › Layout); don't change fonts, colours or the footer.
- The last slide, *Style kit*, has a figure box with caption, numbered points, a stat card and a key-message box to copy. Delete it in the merged deck.
- Figures: export at 300 dpi with a white background; label axes with units (veh/km, veh/h).

## Workflow

1. Copy `template.pptx` to `slides/drafts/<letter>.pptx` on a branch `<letter>/slides`.
2. Fill in your section only and open a PR by **1 Nov**.
3. A merges the sections into `slides/final.pptx` by 3 Nov for the rehearsal, then submits.
